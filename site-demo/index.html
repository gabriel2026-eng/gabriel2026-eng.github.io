What we do

From a single piece to the whole house

We work with solid wood, veneer and painted MDF. Every project starts with measurements taken in your home, not from a catalogue.

Kitchens

How we work

Four steps, no surprises

You know from the start what you get, what it costs and when it arrives. The price in the quote does not change along the way.

  1. A chat and measurements

    We come to you, measure, look at the light and the rest of your furniture, and listen. Free in Cluj-Napoca and the surrounding area.

  2. 3D design and quote

    Within five days you receive 3D renders and a firm quote with materials, finishes and lead time. We adjust it until you love it.

  3. Making it in the workshop

    Four to six weeks of work. You get photos from the workshop every week, so you can watch it take shape.

  4. Delivery and fitting

    We deliver, fit, adjust the doors and drawers and clean up after ourselves. You keep a written 5-year warranty.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most often

Can't find your answer here? Write or call us; we reply within one working day.

How long does it take from the first chat to fitting?

Usually 5–7 weeks: one week for measurements and design, then 4–6 weeks of making. Simple pieces can be faster. The exact date goes in the quote.

How do I pay?

A 30% deposit when you sign the quote, the rest on delivery and fitting. You receive an invoice and a written warranty.

Do you work outside Cluj?

Yes, anywhere in the country. In Cluj-Napoca and within 30 km measurements are free; elsewhere, the travel cost is shown clearly in the quote before you decide.

What happens if there is a problem after fitting?

The 5-year warranty covers the structure and the joints. Write or call us and within 5 working days we come to repair or replace, at no cost to you.
