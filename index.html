AI email assistant that drafts replies

Demo · AI
Wobbly coffee table – no reply for a week Complaint Urgent · English

"One of the legs wobbles, and the table rocks even on a flat floor. I emailed you last week about it and never heard back…"

Hello Daniel,

We're sorry about the wobbly table, and even sorrier that your first email went unanswered for a week. That is not how we want to treat anyone.

A table that rocks on a flat floor is a defect, and we fix it free of charge under our 5-year warranty. We'll send someone to adjust or replace the leg; a visit like this usually happens within 5 working days of your reply.

To plan it, could you send us a short video or photo of the wobble, plus a few days and times when someone will be home? We'll confirm the date the same day.
Check before sending: find the customer's first email (maybe in Spam) and reply today; confirm a fitter is free within 5 working days.
Dining table for 8 – rough price? Price question Normal · English

"I'm looking for a solid oak dining table that seats 8 people, roughly 240 x 100 cm. Could you give me a rough idea of the price and how long it would take?"

Hi Sarah,

A solid oak dining table for 8 at around 240 × 100 cm usually costs between 4,500 and 7,500 lei (VAT included), depending on the exact size, the thickness of the top, the legs and the finish. The final price goes into a written quote and doesn't change afterwards.

You receive a 3D design and a fixed quote within 5 working days of the measuring visit, and the table is built in 4–6 weeks from approval. Measuring visits are free in Cluj-Napoca…
Check before sending: confirm availability for the measuring visit; add travel costs if the address is outside the usual area.

The assistant reads incoming customer emails and prepares, for each one, a category, a priority and a draft reply in the customer's own language. It uses only your company's information (opening hours, price ranges, lead times, warranty) and never invents a price or a promise; what it isn't sure about goes into a "check before sending" note.

  • Categories: price question, complaint, booking, order status, partnership, other
  • Priorities with the reason, so the inbox is handled in the right order
  • Drafts land in your Drafts folder (Gmail or Outlook); a person reads and sends
  • Customer emails are treated as data, never as instructions; failed emails are reported, not lost
  • Spending cap and a daily limit on the API; your own API key, revocable any time

Python, Claude API (structured output); Gmail via Apps Script or Gmail API; Outlook via Power Automate or Microsoft Graph
